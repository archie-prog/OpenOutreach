How {{ workspace_name|default:"Grantgunner" }} works

  1. Connect a LinkedIn account — Accounts tab → add the email + password. If the account uses 2FA, paste its TOTP secret so the worker can sign in unattended. Set per-account daily caps and a send window (we recommend ≤ 30 connects/day).
  2. Tell the AI what you sell — AI Context tab → add your API key and a clear description of your offer and ideal lead. This powers lead scoring, AI search and message personalisation.
  3. Build a sequence — Flows tab → a branching playbook: connect → wait → message → InMail. The accepted branch runs when they connect; a reply always stops the sequence. Use {first_name}, {company} etc. to personalise.
  4. Add leads — Leads tab → import a CSV, paste a LinkedIn search, or describe your ideal lead and let the AI find them. Each list fills toward a target you set.
  5. Launch a campaign — Campaigns tab → pick a sequence + a lead list and activate. Each person is contacted at most once across all campaigns. Pause/resume any time.
  6. Watch & reply — the Dashboard shows connections, acceptances, messages and replies over time. Replies land in the Unibox — reply by hand, and turn on Slack pings in AI Context so you never miss one.

⚖️ Use responsibly. Keep volumes human, only contact people relevant to your offer, and stay within LinkedIn's terms. The daily caps, randomisation and send windows exist to protect your account — don't crank them past what a person could plausibly do.
